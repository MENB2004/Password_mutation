## Interpretation

The workflow now separates parameter selection from final evaluation. The reported metrics
describe this independent synthetic experiment only. They do not establish real-world
password-cracking prediction performance.

The conventional baseline is a transparent project-specific length/character-diversity
score, not an industry-standard password meter.

## Limitations

- The validation data are synthetic.
- Labels are generated by construction.
- The dictionary and mutation rules are limited.
- PSI has not been validated against real cracking times or breach outcomes.

## Next step

Build the final research application and report, while clearly labelling synthetic results
as prototype evidence. If a properly licensed real research dataset becomes available,
repeat this exact calibration/held-out protocol on that dataset.
